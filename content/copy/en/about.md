## ABOUT-1

I studied psychology, with minors in mathematics and statistics, then took a master's in data science. I started a PhD at Georgia Tech combining biomedical engineering and AI, and left it. I now work at Accenture as an AI developer and consultant, building data science and AI solutions for the US Marshals Service, part of the Department of Justice. I live in Atlanta, and since 2024 I have run the Atlanta Korean Data Science Community, a Korean-language data-science meetup — one session a month.

## ABOUT-2

My time goes into systems where teams of AI agents do the actual work. At an algorithmic trading company I work as PM and designed the harness the agents run under; on my own I run fantasy football, personal finance, and combined training scheduling the same way. Doyak, a nonprofit for Korean families living alongside addiction, is being set up by a team of eleven agent roles as well. The domains differ, but three structures repeat: one agent's conclusion has to be attacked by another, exactly one role holds a veto, and rules are enforced by hooks rather than requested in a document. Alongside those are five apps I built and shipped that are still up.
