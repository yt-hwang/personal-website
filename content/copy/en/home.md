## SEC4-TITLE

How the work is set up

## METHOD-INTRO

Trading, fantasy football, personal finance, a weekly training plan, a nonprofit,
and this page. The domains have nothing to do with each other. What is underneath
them is the same, and this is it.

## METHOD-1-TITLE

Adversarial verification

## METHOD-1

One agent's conclusion does not move forward until another agent has attacked it
item by item. In fantasy football the strategist marks the optimizer's squad PASS
or FAIL; at Renfinity the verification role runs its own tests on whatever
strategy proposes.

## METHOD-2-TITLE

A single veto

## METHOD-2

Exactly one role per team holds a veto, and no other role can override it. In the
finance system it is the risk manager, in the training system the recovery
manager, and on this site the privacy reviewer — which is why some cards here
have no link.

## METHOD-3-TITLE

Enforced by hooks

## METHOD-3

Rules are enforced by hooks, not requested in a document. A guard that fails
closed stops the work where it stands, and the gate in front of execution will
not open until the numbers behind the decision are filled in.

## SEC6-TITLE

Community and talks

## AKDS-SEC

There was no place in Atlanta to talk about data and AI in Korean. Plenty of
material exists online; almost nobody says what it was actually like to use the
thing. So the community has one rule: you come with something you tried, not
something you read. It has met every month since 2024 and has not skipped one.
In May 2026 I ran a hands-on session, "Harness Engineering: building systems
that tame and control AI agents" — the same problem every system on this page
was built to solve. In August, "Claude Code × Telegram: building your own AI
agent team," which is the short version of how each of them starts. Outside
speakers cover what the room does not already know: security, UX design. And
for the people who wanted to stop listening and start building, a Kaggle cohort
ran alongside the sessions.

## META-home-desc

Six systems in which teams of AI agents do the actual work, and five apps in production. The same structure repeats across trading, personal finance, training, and a nonprofit.

## META-renfinity

Renfinity — a five-agent AI team runs an algorithmic trading system day to day, with rules enforced by fail-closed hooks and a three-stage document pipeline.

## META-fantasy-epl

Fantasy EPL Automation Harness — eight agents decide each gameweek's transfers, lineup, captain, and chips through a generate-then-verify loop and two hard gates.

## META-training-coach

Personal Training Coach — five coach agents fold running, lifting, HYROX, and jiu-jitsu into one weekly schedule, with a recovery manager reconciling total load.

## META-doyak

Doyak — a Korean-language mutual-support hub for families and children living alongside addiction, run in setup by a team of eleven AI agents.

## META-this-site

This Site — the portfolio itself is the output of a six-agent team. Every value carries a source path, and the privacy reviewer holds a deploy veto.

## META-humor-test

Humor Type Test — find which of 16 humor types is yours and share it as an image. Per-platform sharing and server-generated OG images.

## META-bible-reading

Today's Word — a Bible-reading app where a group opens the same page and sees whether everyone read today. Invite-link rooms and daily reminders.

## META-akds

Atlanta Korean Data Science Community — monthly since 2024. Not a place to be taught, a place to share what we actually tried.
